# Triage — version 1

Plan the review from the worker-computed change statistics, baseline/follow-up facts, Jira requirements and available source context. Assess size, behavioral impact, collateral effects, sensitivity and complexity. Tests/fixtures excluded from effective size still remain inspectable.

Choose only supplied model/effort combinations and stay within the reviewer cap. Assign every mandatory lens by its configured ID without replacing its instructions. Return the triage output schema; do not echo change statistics or assign reviewer identities. If requirements cannot fit the plan, expose the conflict rather than omitting coverage.
